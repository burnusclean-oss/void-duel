# Void Duel

A free, fan-made top-down space shooter that runs in your web browser. It's a tribute to Silent Death Online, the arena space combat game from Mythic Entertainment that we played as kids and that shut down in 2001.

We're rebuilding it from memory, with no original code or art. We're doing this for fun and to learn, and we're not affiliated with the original creators or publishers.

## What we're building

- Fast arena dogfights in open space, with ships that drift and glide
- Four rival houses and team-based modes
- A credits and rank system, where you earn credits from kills and wins and spend them on gear
- Customizable ships: hull, shield, repair bot, engine, energy weapon and autocannons
- Heat and energy limits: your engine decides how fast the autocannons overheat and how fast energy comes back

## Controls

- W, A, S, D: move
- Mouse: aim
- Left click: energy weapon (or Space)
- Right click: autocannons (or E)
- Shift: afterburner
- R: repair bot
